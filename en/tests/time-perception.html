    What is the Time Perception Test?

    This test asks you to judge how much time has passed without looking at a clock. Psychologists call this "time perception." The brain doesn't run on an exact clock - it relies on a rough "internal clock" that can drift from real time depending on what you're focused on or doing.

    Error guide

    ErrorRating

    Over 1.0sFree spirit

    How is it measured?

    A random amount of time between 1 and 10 seconds passes, then the screen changes. Without seeing any numbers or clock, you type in how many seconds you think passed. The smaller the gap between your guess and the real elapsed time, the better your score. Up to 8 players can compete in the same round.

    Can I train my time sense?

    A lot of people count a steady beat in their head ("one, two, three...") or use their breathing rhythm to track time. That said, some argue this measures counting ability more than pure time perception - if you want to test the real thing, try guessing without counting at all.
