What is jitter clicking?

Unlike normal clicking, which moves only the finger, jitter clicking tenses the forearm and wrist so they vibrate rapidly, and channels that vibration into the mouse button. It's widely used in the Minecraft PvP community, where skilled players push well past normal clicking (6–8 CPS) into the 10–14 CPS range.

What is click consistency? (and a safety note)

This test also calculates the standard deviation of your click intervals and reports it as click consistency. A smaller number means a more regular vibration. Keep in mind that jitter clicking puts significant stress on the wrist and arm, so repeated practice isn't recommended — stop right away if you feel any pain.
