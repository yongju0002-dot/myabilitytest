Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

MyAbilityTest ("this site") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the site collects, and how that information is used and stored.

1. There are no user accounts

This site has no sign-up or login. We do not collect or store personally identifying information such as your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.

2. Test records are stored only on your device

Your scores, session statistics, and all-time record lists are saved exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data is never transmitted to our servers, and the site operator cannot see it.

  • Stored where: your own browser
  • Transmitted: never sent to a server
  • How to delete: clearing your browser's site data (cookies and site data) removes all records immediately

This means the "All-Time Top Records" list is not a leaderboard shared with other users — it only collects the scores you set on that particular device.

3. Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service, to cover operating costs.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • To opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies more broadly, visit aboutads.info.

For details on how Google collects and processes data for advertising, see Google's privacy and terms for partner sites.

4. Access logs and traffic data

This site is hosted through Cloudflare. To deliver the service and protect it against abusive traffic, standard access data such as IP address, browser type, and request time may be processed at the infrastructure level. This is not used to identify individual visitors.

This site also uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which tests they use. It sets cookies to collect anonymous statistics such as page views, test starts and completions, and the mode selected; this information is sent to and stored by Google. IP addresses are anonymised by default in Google Analytics 4, and no personally identifying information such as names or email addresses is collected. Your test scores and leaderboard records are never sent to analytics — they stay in your browser. You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

5. Audio and input device access

The Hearing Age Test uses the browser's Web Audio API to play tones. It does not access or record your microphone. Tests that measure clicks and key presses use only the count and timing of inputs made on that page; the content of your input is never stored or transmitted.

6. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Nature of the test results

All tests on this site are for entertainment and general reference. Results vary with your device, monitor refresh rate, input hardware, headphone quality, surroundings, and how alert you are, and they are not a substitute for medical diagnosis or professional assessment. If you have concerns about your hearing, vision, or cognition, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, email yongju0002@gmail.com.
