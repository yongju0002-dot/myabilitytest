    What is the Time Stop game?

    Stop the clock the instant it hits your target time (5, 10, or 15 seconds - your choice). You count the time in your head without looking at any numbers, then hit the button the moment you think the target has been reached. The smaller the gap between the real elapsed time and your target, the better your score.

    Error guide

    ErrorRating
    Under 0.05sTime-sense superpower
    0.05 - 0.15sPrecise timer
    0.15 - 0.3sAverage sense
    0.3 - 0.6sA bit off
    Over 0.6sFree spirit

    How is this different from the Time Perception Test?

    The Time Perception Test asks you to judge time that has already passed, after the fact. This game asks you to hit a timing target in real time, while the clock is still running - which brings prediction and reaction into play together.

    Why are there multiple target times?

    The longer the target (5s vs. 10s vs. 15s), the more your perceived error tends to grow. Nailing a short stop and holding steady through a longer one draw on slightly different skills, so it's worth trying all three. Up to 8 players can compete in the same round.
