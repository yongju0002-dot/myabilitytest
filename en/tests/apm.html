What is APM (Actions Per Minute)? It's the number of actions — mouse clicks plus key presses — you perform in one minute. It became widely known as a measure of pro-player skill in StarCraft, and it's still used as a yardstick for control speed in RTS and MOBA games. StarCraft pros are generally reported to average somewhere around 300–400 APM during matches.

That said, high APM doesn't automatically mean high skill. Meaningless repeated inputs inflate the number just as easily, so what really matters is the share of actions that accomplish something. This test graphs your APM in 10-second segments, so you can also see whether your speed fades toward the end.
