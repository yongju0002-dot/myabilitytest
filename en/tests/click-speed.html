What's a good CPS score?

4-6 CPS is typical. 8+ is fast, and 10+ is very fast. Above 12 CPS is this site's top grade (SS).

How is CPS calculated?

CPS (Clicks Per Second) is your click count divided by the time in seconds. For example, 80 clicks in 10 seconds is 8.0 CPS. This site's grade bands aren't from an official published study — they're our own scale, calibrated against the general sense of speed reported across click-speed test sites.

Why do shorter tests show a higher CPS?

A very short test like 1-3 seconds only captures a quick burst of speed, so it tends to score higher than a sustained test of 10 seconds or more — your fingers fatigue the longer you click, which naturally pulls the average down. That's why this page tracks records separately per duration.

Can I train to click faster?

Alternating your index and middle finger ("drag clicking") or vibrating your forearm ("jitter clicking") are widely known community techniques. You can measure each one separately: Jitter Click Test, Kohi Click Test.
