What is dynamic vision? It's the ability to instantly catch and recognize something that's moving or appears and vanishes quickly. This test measures how fast you can read information within a very short exposure window rather than actual motion, but the underlying skill is similar. It's a big factor when tracking a fast ball in sports like soccer or baseball, or spotting an enemy that flashes across your screen for a split second in an FPS game.

Dynamic vision is partly natural, but it's also believed to improve somewhat through repeated visual training and experience with games and sports. That said, results here depend on your screen's refresh rate, device performance, and how alert you are — so treat it as a fun estimate.
