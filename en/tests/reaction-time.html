What is a reaction time test?

A reaction time test measures, in milliseconds (ms), how quickly you click after visually noticing a color change on screen. The lower the number, the faster your reaction speed. It's a common indicator of split-second responsiveness used in gaming and sports.

What's the average reaction time in ms?

Visual reaction time typically falls in the 250–300ms range for most people. Low 200s is faster than average, and 150–200ms is a well-trained level. Results can shift by 10–50ms depending on your device, monitor refresh rate, and touchscreen latency.

How to measure accurately

A single try is less reliable than an average of 5+ tries. Cold hands, drowsiness, wireless mice, or mobile touch input can all add extra delay — repeat under the same device/environment to compare fairly. Clicking before the color actually changes won't give you a true reaction time reading.
