Why measure spacebar speed?

Plenty of games bind jump or dash to the spacebar, so how fast you can repeat that input translates directly into control. Unlike mouse clicking, many people hit the bar with a thumb or the side of the palm, which is why spacebar results tend to look different from CPS results.

How is this measured?

This test ignores the auto-repeat that fires when you hold the key down, so only genuine press-and-release actions count. Your keyboard's switch type (membrane vs. mechanical) and key travel distance can also change your result.
