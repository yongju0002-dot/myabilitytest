What is mouse accuracy?

Unlike clicking a target precisely (flicking), this test looks at your ability to keep the cursor on an intended path. It's the control you need for tracking aim in FPS games, pen work in graphics software, and any drag operation where following exactly is the point.

How is the score calculated?

Your score combines the share of time you stayed inside the path (accuracy) with how quickly you finished. Move too fast and you'll drift off the track; move too cautiously and the clock runs, so balancing speed against precision is the whole challenge. If your mouse sensitivity is very high, fine adjustments get harder — it can be worth dialing it down.
